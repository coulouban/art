Coulouban

Light is his name

Lover of nature and its saints, of nature and its kin, Coulouban walks with admiration on the path that is his own.

Strewn with tragic logs, sacred menhirs and magical beings, the winding road gently leads him towards the only conceivable end, the peace of freedom.

Passionate about life, it is the divine gardens that inspire in him the redeeming breath, full of courage and honesty, that characterizes him so much.

Born in hell with the name of light, the journey was thus announced, like a subtle mixture of order and chaos.

Coulouban is the entire cosmos inhabiting his own skin, it is the sun shining while casting its shadow.

A lover in the moonlight, no pen can make him fly higher than the love he feels up there, in his heart.

The infinity of the Eternal mystery fascinates him, amazes him and surprises him every time his eye blinks and opens.

It is this deep song that he strives to simply transmit through his complex art.

Poetry is his first creation.
Painting is his favorite meditation.
Music is his way of living.

In his own unique way, he moves and the movement carries him away like a gentle wave of sea water.

Coulouban doesn't play music, he picks up his instrument, and the divine breath does the rest.
