The Transmuted Instrument

Coulouban's accordion is no longer a fairground or party instrument. In his hands, it has become a diffuser of frequencies, a gentle weapon deployed against modern chaos.

This music does not seek to entertain but to envelop. The notes stretch, breathe, and create a space where time seems suspended.

An Experience of Clarity

Seated on his stool, walking among the stars or surrounded by his canvases, Coulouban transforms the atmosphere. In his concerts, space expands and time seems to stop.

The audience is invited to breathe. Seated or lying down, in the dark or with eyes closed, the spectator plunges into a hypnotic state. It is a collective meditation where sound comes to illuminate the shadows.

The Call of the Sky

Hybrid and autonomous, this acoustic concert adapts to places of grace : resonant chapels, dawn gardens, art galleries, or outdoor spaces.

It is a pause. An invitation to rediscover, during a listening, the depth and sacred mystery of our own existence.
