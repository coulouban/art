The Word

The ink and
the breath.

Naming the invisible

Before note and color, there is the need to give a name. Writing is the first foundation, the first echo of the cave.

Words are not mere reflections of Coulouban's thought—they are its origin. They map his inner visions and trace the contours of the frequencies he is about to play.

Fragments of the soul

"The human being

Is like a well,

Without a bottom.


Do not try to fill it,

Then it will fill itself.


And of its own accord,

The spring will gush forth.".
