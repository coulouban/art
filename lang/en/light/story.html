Genesis

The Metamorphosis
of Silence.

The belly of the rock

Many years ago, the world's turmoil became too heavy. Coulouban withdrew behind the rock to seek what was no longer heard on the surface.

In the absolute silence of a cave, stripped of clocks and landmarks, he began an intimate conversation with silence. There, in that fertile dusk bathed by a divine light, Coulouban was born. A secluded retreat where the instrument ceased to be an object and became an extension of his soul—a soul he learns to love day by day.

The necessity of sound

In three days, cradled by his first accordion, he opened a breach in space-time. The time of learning was swept away by the necessity of breath.

Since that day, he no longer plays music in the classical sense : he lets it pass through him. He becomes the bridge between the cave's silence and the chaos of the modern world, transforming the accordion into a diffuser of harmonic frequencies.

An invisible orchestra

What he hears in the secret of his mind is a mathematical symmetry, a language that words sometimes fail to contain. This is where the rest of his work begins.

If he doesn't play it, he writes it. If he doesn't write it, he paints it.
